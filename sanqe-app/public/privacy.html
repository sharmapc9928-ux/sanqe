Fedge Sanqe

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Fedge Sanqe (“the app”, “we”) is a practice app for bank-exam aspirants (SBI PO/Clerk, IBPS PO/Clerk/RRB, RBI and similar). Some topics are free; the rest need a paid Practice plan or a coaching's course. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it. We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or advertising ID. It does not see or store card, UPI or bank login details.

Who else sees it

  • Other users see only your first name, score and time on the Daily Challenge leaderboard. Nothing else about you is visible to other users.
  • Your coaching — if you join a coaching's test series (with its access code, for free, or by buying it), that coaching sees your name and email, the code you used, when you joined, and your answers, scores, ranks and times on its mocks. It sees nothing else from the app (not your practice history, Daily Challenge or descriptive answers). Other students in the series see only their own rank and the topper and average scores, not your name. The coaching handles what it sees under its own terms with you; it can remove you from its series.
  • Supabase (database and sign-in; servers in Mumbai, India) stores your account and app data on our behalf.
  • Google Gemini API receives the text of a descriptive test when you submit it for evaluation, together with the question it answers. No name, email or account ID is sent with it. Google processes it under the Gemini API terms. If you don't want your writing processed this way, don't submit descriptive tests.
  • Google (only if you use “Continue with Google”) to verify your sign-in.
  • Razorpay (when you buy a Practice plan or a test series, or when a coaching pays for its portal plan) processes the payment under Razorpay's privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts the app and provides the anonymous, cookie-free usage statistics described above.
  • Google Fonts serves the app's typefaces; your browser requests them directly from Google.

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We may disclose data if required by law.

Data kept on your device

The app stores a few things in your browser's local storage so it works smoothly: your theme and language choice, your best Circle Game score, and in-progress answers for a running test (so a refresh doesn't lose them). These stay on your device and are cleared when you finish the test or clear the app's data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, so your progress history stays available. A coaching's portal payment records are kept while its account exists. Records of purchases you made (Practice plans and test series) are kept for accounting and tax purposes even after your account is deleted, but without any link to you. Contact form messages are kept until we've dealt with them and for up to 12 months after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Anonymous usage statistics are kept in aggregate form only.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open your profile (the person icon, top-right) → Delete my account → type DELETE to confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it: target exam, practice history, Mistake Notebook, Daily Challenge results, descriptive drafts, answers and evaluations, coaching mock attempts and series memberships, question reports, any partner application, and — if you run a coaching account — that coaching with its test series, mocks, questions, codes, payment records and its students' results on them. Records of what you bought (amount, plan, dates, Razorpay IDs) are kept without your account link for accounting. Messages you sent from the Contact page are kept without your account link; ask us and we'll delete them too.

Without the app: email rohit32860505@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Fedge Sanqe account”. We will delete the account and all associated data within 7 days and confirm by email.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over who are preparing for competitive exams. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect, and update the app's Play Store data-safety section in the same release.
